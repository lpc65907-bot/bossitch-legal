购买与恢复购买

  • 武器、动作、功能特权、现金衣橱款式等非消耗型商品在购买后绑定当前 Apple 账户。重装或换机后，可在游戏商店内点击“恢复购买”。
  • 现金衣橱款式为一次买断：购买后所有领导永久可用，不需要为另一个领导重复购买。
  • 领导槽与入场台词额外更换额度是消耗型商品，按次使用，不跨重装或换设备恢复。
  • 价格、实际扣款币种和税费以 App Store 购买确认页为准。
  • 退款由 Apple 处理。请访问 reportaproblem.apple.com 提交退款请求。

如何删除我的数据

  • 在游戏内删除领导、录音或照片后，对应内容会从本机移除。
  • 卸载游戏会删除本机保存的档案、设置、照片、背景图、录音和反馈队列，且无法从游戏内恢复。
  • 购买记录由 Apple 保存；卸载后重新安装，可使用“恢复购买”找回符合条件的非消耗型商品。
  • 当前版本没有账号系统，也没有运营方服务器存档。

照片、背景图与录音权限

  • 只有在你主动使用照片换脸、自定义背景或录音功能时，游戏才会请求系统照片或麦克风权限。
  • 拒绝权限不会影响其他游戏功能。
  • 你可以随时在 iOS“设置 → 隐私与安全性”中撤销照片或麦克风权限；已保存的内容需要在游戏内删除或卸载应用。
  • 录音默认静音；播放外放前会再次请求确认。

反馈

游戏设置内的反馈入口会把内容保存在本机队列。当前版本未连接公开反馈服务器，因此不会自动上传。若你需要立即联系我们，请直接发送邮件至 pcwlfeedback@gmail.com。

常见问题

商店显示“价格暂不可用”或商品没有加载

请确认设备已连接网络、已登录 Apple 账户，并稍后重试。若使用 TestFlight 或沙盒环境，请确认已登录对应的沙盒账户。仍无法加载时，请通过邮件告知游戏版本和截图。

恢复购买没有找回消耗型商品

领导槽和入场台词额外更换额度属于消耗型商品，Apple 不提供跨重装或换设备的恢复。非消耗型商品可在同一 Apple 账户下恢复。

游戏需要联网吗？

核心游戏为单机体验。App Store 商品加载、购买和恢复购买需要网络与 Apple 服务。

如何反馈新武器、新动作或新皮肤？

请使用游戏内反馈入口，或发送邮件至 pcwlfeedback@gmail.com。

Support

Need help?

Check the FAQ below first. If the issue remains, email pcwlfeedback@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, game version, and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Email supportPrivacy Policy

Purchases and restoring

  • Non-consumable items such as weapons, actions, feature privileges, and cash wardrobe styles are tied to the Apple account used for the purchase. After reinstalling or changing devices, use “Restore Purchases” in the in-game store.
  • Cash wardrobe styles are a one-time buyout: after purchase they are permanently available for every boss; you do not need to buy the same style again for another boss.
  • Boss slots and extra entrance-line changes are consumable items, used per purchase, and are not restored across reinstalls or devices.
  • Prices, currency, and taxes are shown on the App Store purchase confirmation sheet.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. Submit a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How to delete my data

  • Deleting a boss, recording, or photo in the game removes the related content from your device.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the local profile, settings, photos, backgrounds, recordings, and feedback queue. This cannot be undone from inside the game.
  • Purchase records are held by Apple. After reinstalling, “Restore Purchases” can recover eligible non-consumable items.
  • The current version has no account system and no operator server save.

Photo, background, and microphone permissions

  • The game requests photo or microphone permission only when you actively use photo face, custom background, or recording features.
  • Denying permission does not affect other features.
  • You can revoke photo or microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Saved content must be deleted in the game or by uninstalling the app.
  • Recordings play muted by default; the game asks again before playing aloud.

Feedback

The in-game feedback entry stores content in a local queue. The current version is not connected to a public feedback server, so it does not upload automatically. To contact us immediately, email pcwlfeedback@gmail.com.

FAQ

The store shows “price unavailable” or products do not load

Check your network connection and Apple account, then try again. In TestFlight or sandbox builds, confirm that you are signed in with the appropriate sandbox account. If the problem persists, email us with the game version and a screenshot.

Restore Purchases did not recover a consumable item

Boss slots and extra entrance-line changes are consumable items; Apple does not restore them across reinstalls or devices. Non-consumable items can be restored under the same Apple account.

Does the game require an internet connection?

The core game is a single-player experience. Loading App Store products, purchasing, and restoring purchases require a network connection and Apple services.

How do I suggest a new weapon, action, or skin?

Use the in-game feedback entry or email pcwlfeedback@gmail.com.
