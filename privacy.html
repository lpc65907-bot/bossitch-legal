隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-09 · 运营主体：Liu Pengcheng（个人开发者） · 联系邮箱：pcwlfeedback@gmail.com

简要说明

BOSSITCH 是一款单机娱乐游戏。当前版本没有账号系统，也不向运营方服务器上传游戏档案、照片、背景图、录音或反馈。下面说明游戏在设备本地处理哪些信息、为什么处理，以及你可以如何删除它们。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 iOS 应用 BOSSITCH（App Store 名称：Whack Your Boss - Relax）及其内置功能。使用本游戏即表示你已阅读本政策。若你不同意，请停止使用并卸载游戏。

2. 游戏在本地处理的信息

  • 你主动提供的内容：领导姓名或昵称、求饶与入场台词、照片、背景图、录音、反馈正文及可选联系方式。
  • 游戏生成的记录：游戏进度、设置、语言、当前外观、权益与购买记录、协议接受记录，以及设备本地生成的玩家标识。
  • 技术信息：游戏不会为分析或广告目的收集设备标识、位置、通讯录或使用统计。反馈上传功能在当前版本未连接公开服务器。

照片、背景图和录音只在你主动使用相应功能时，通过系统照片选择器或麦克风授权读取。游戏不会在后台读取相册、麦克风、通讯录或位置。

3. 处理目的与依据

上述信息仅用于提供和保存游戏体验、个性化领导、恢复购买、处理你主动提交的反馈、保障本机数据安全和排查故障。处理依据包括履行你请求的游戏功能、你的同意（例如录音授权）以及适用的法律义务。

4. 保存位置与期限

游戏档案、设置、照片、背景图、录音和反馈队列保存在当前设备的应用容器中。当前版本不提供账号同步或云存档。你可以随时在游戏内删除领导、录音或照片；卸载游戏或清除应用数据会删除本机保存的全部相关内容。

当前版本的反馈队列只保存在本机，不会自动上传。若未来启用公开反馈服务，本政策会更新，并在服务端最多保留反馈数据 12 个月，期满删除或匿名化，法律另有要求的除外。

5. 共享、委托与跨境

我们不出售你的个人信息，也不向广告、分析或数据经纪服务提供信息。使用 App Store 购买时，Apple 负责处理支付和交易；游戏只接收并保存核验交易和发放权益所需的交易标识、商品 ID、环境和权益状态，不接触银行卡信息。除你的同意、法律要求或提供游戏功能所必需外，我们不会向无关第三方提供你的个人信息。

6. 你的权利

你可以依法请求查阅、复制、更正、补充或删除个人信息，撤回同意，或通过游戏内入口删除内容。你也可以发送邮件至 pcwlfeedback@gmail.com。卸载游戏会删除本机数据；撤回同意不影响撤回前已进行的处理。

7. 未成年人

游戏面向年满 18 周岁的用户。我们不会明知收集未成年人的个人信息；如发现误收集，会尽快删除。若你所在地要求更高年龄，从其规定。

8. 安全

我们采取合理的技术和管理措施保护信息，包括本机访问控制、最小化收集和必要的传输校验。任何系统都无法保证绝对安全；如发生安全事件，我们会按法律要求处理并告知。

9. 第三方链接

游戏内可能包含素材许可或第三方网站链接。打开这些链接后，适用对应网站自己的隐私政策，本政策不覆盖第三方网站的处理行为。

10. 政策更新

我们可能因功能、法律或平台规则变化更新本政策，并在本页面公布。重大变更会在生效前提示；需要重新同意时，我们会在游戏内再次征求你的同意。

11. 联系我们

隐私问题、权利请求或投诉请联系：pcwlfeedback@gmail.com。我们会在合理期限内处理。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09 · Operator: Liu Pengcheng (individual developer) · Contact: pcwlfeedback@gmail.com

Summary

BOSSITCH is a single-player entertainment game. The current version has no account system and does not upload game profiles, photos, backgrounds, recordings, or feedback to an operator server. This page explains what the game processes locally, why, and how you can delete it.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS app BOSSITCH (App Store name: Whack Your Boss - Relax) and its built-in features. By using the game you confirm that you have read this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the game and uninstall it.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Content you provide: boss names or nicknames, begging and entrance lines, photos, background images, recordings, feedback text, and an optional contact address.
  • Game-generated records: progress, settings, language, current appearance, entitlements and purchase records, consent records, and a locally generated player identifier.
  • Technical information: the game does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage analytics for advertising or analytics purposes. The feedback upload feature is not connected to a public server in the current version.

Photos, background images, and recordings are read only when you actively use the relevant feature, through the system photo picker or microphone permission. The game does not read your photo library, microphone, contacts, or location in the background.

3. Purposes and legal bases

This information is used only to provide and save the game experience, personalize bosses, restore purchases, process feedback you voluntarily submit, protect local data, and troubleshoot. Processing is based on performing the game features you request, your consent (for example, recording permission), and applicable legal obligations.

4. Storage location and retention

Game profiles, settings, photos, backgrounds, recordings, and the feedback queue are stored in the app container on your current device. The current version does not provide account sync or cloud saves. You can delete bosses, recordings, or photos in the game at any time; uninstalling the game or clearing app data deletes all related local content.

The current feedback queue stays on your device and is not uploaded automatically. If a public feedback service is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated, and feedback data will be retained on the server for no more than 12 months, then deleted or anonymized, unless law requires otherwise.

5. Sharing and cross-border transfer

We do not sell your personal information or provide it to advertising, analytics, or data-broker services. When you purchase through the App Store, Apple processes payment and transactions; the game receives and stores only the transaction identifier, product ID, environment, and entitlement status needed to verify the transaction and grant the content. The game does not receive card details. We do not provide your personal information to unrelated third parties except with your consent, as required by law, or as necessary to provide the game features.

6. Your rights

You may request access, a copy, correction, supplementation, or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent, or delete content through the in-game entry. You may also email pcwlfeedback@gmail.com. Uninstalling the game deletes local data; withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

7. Minors

The game is intended for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors and will delete it promptly if we learn of such collection. If your jurisdiction requires a higher age, that requirement applies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including local access controls, data minimization, and necessary transmission checks. No system can guarantee absolute security; if a security incident occurs, we will handle it and notify as required by law.

9. Third-party links

The game may include asset-license or third-party website links. Once you open those links, the privacy policy of the relevant website applies; this policy does not cover third-party websites.

10. Changes

We may update this policy due to changes in features, law, or platform rules and will publish the update on this page. Material changes will be flagged before they take effect; if renewed consent is required, we will ask again in the game.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, rights requests, or complaints, contact pcwlfeedback@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.
